**Messages** is an encrypted chat with your team.

- **Start a topic thread** on a Project.
- Click **Discuss** on an approval, request, or update to talk about that exact item.
- See **how quickly** the team usually replies.
- See **Seen by the team** once they have read your message.

<Note>
Message emails never include the message text. They only tell you there is something new.
</Note>
