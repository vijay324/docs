Open **Projects** to see a card for each Project: progress, health, a health note, dates, and counts.

## On a Project page

| Section | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| **Kickoff card** | Your team, the first milestones, and how fast they usually reply |
| **Delivery card** | Shown instead when the Project is complete |
| **Roadmap** | Milestones, with moved dates and the reason |
| **Status updates** | The latest open, older ones collapsed |
| **Timeline** | A dated feed of what happened |
| **Files, meetings, requests, documents** | Everything shared with you |
| **Shared credentials** | Logins the team chose to share |

## Status updates and timeline

Your team posts short updates: a headline, health, what is done, what is next, risks, and decisions needed. The **timeline** records updates posted, milestones completed, files shared, contracts signed, meetings held, and requests added to the plan.

## What you will and will not see

- **Progress** appears only if your team shares it.
- **Hidden Projects** do not appear at all.
- **Task names and assignees** are never shown.
