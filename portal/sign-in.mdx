Your team sends you sign-in details by email or WhatsApp. It takes a minute to get started.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the portal link">
    The sign-in page shows your service provider's name and logo.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your username and temporary password">
    You can use your email in place of the username.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your own password">
    You must choose a new password before you continue.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Some older invitations use a **magic link** instead. Open the link to sign in.
</Note>

## Trouble signing in?

Ask your contact at the team to **resend** your sign-in details. They can also reset access from their side.
