## Contact roles

Every client contact has one of two roles.

| Role | Can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Approver** (default) | Accept, sign, approve, and answer questions |
| **Viewer** | Read everything shared. Can still upload requested files and confirm delivery |

Admins and Managers set roles from [Portal access](/clients/portal-access).

## Branding by plan

| Plan | What the portal shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Free** | Flotick branding |
| **Starter** | "Powered by Flotick" |
| **Pro and Max** | **White-label**: your organization's name and logo, including in emails |

## Custom domain

<Badge color="purple">Pro+</Badge> <Badge color="gray">Admin or Manager</Badge>

Serve the portal from your own address, such as `client.yourcompany.com`. The standard portal address keeps working. Set it up in [Domain and verification](/admin/domain-verification).

## Staff preview

Your team can open a read-only, **30-minute preview** (**View as client**). A banner shows it is a preview, and it does not count as a client visit.
