Open a Project and choose **Requests**.

## Ask for a change

Add a **title** and **details**. You can submit up to **20 requests a day**.

## Answer and upload

Your team may ask you a question or request a file. Answer or upload right from the request.

## Track progress

Filter by **All**, **Waiting on you**, **Open**, or **Closed**. Two helpful signals:

- **Seen by the team**: they have read your request.
- **Added to the plan**: the team scheduled the work.

<Note>
**Viewer** contacts can still upload requested files.
</Note>
