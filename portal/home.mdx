The **Welcome** page is your starting point. It shows what needs your attention first, then what is happening.

## Waiting on you

A count appears next to **Welcome** in the menu. The list shows, with **overdue items first**:

- **Documents** to accept or sign
- **Approvals**
- **Questions** to answer
- **Files** the team asked you for

It appears on Welcome and on each Project page.

<Note>
**Viewer** contacts see "Waiting for an approver at your company" instead of decision buttons. See [Contact roles](/portal/roles-and-branding).
</Note>

## Also on Welcome

- The **welcome document**
- **Upcoming meetings**
- **Deadlines and due items** (the next five)
- The **latest status update** for up to three Projects
- **Recent activity**

## How is the project going?

After a milestone is approved or a Project is completed, you may see a short question: **How is the project going?** Give a score from 1 to 5 with an optional comment, or dismiss it. Low scores alert the project lead and Admins so they can help quickly.
