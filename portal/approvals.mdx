When your team needs your go-ahead, it appears in **Waiting on you**. Open it to decide.

## Your choices

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review what was sent">
    A **milestone** or a **specific file version**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve, or request changes">
    To request changes, add a comment. It becomes a change request for the team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Type your **full name** and confirm you are authorized to decide.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Good to know

- Each approval can be decided **only once**.
- You get **reminders** until you respond.
- Only **Approver** contacts can decide. **Viewers** can read. See [Contact roles](/portal/roles-and-branding).

<Tip>
Not sure? Click **Discuss** to start a conversation with the team about this approval.
</Tip>
