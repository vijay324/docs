## Download

Open **Files** (or a Project page) to find files your team shared with you. They are grouped by **Project** and **milestone**.

## Upload

You can upload files to a Project. Each file can be up to **50 MB**, and you can upload up to **50 a day**. If your team requested a file, upload it right from **Waiting on you**.

## Versions

If your team's plan includes version history, you can see **earlier versions** of a file and which version was **approved**.
