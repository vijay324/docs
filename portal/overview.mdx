The Client Portal is where your clients see what is happening on their Projects, without chasing you for updates. Each client signs in to the portal for **one organization**.

<Info>
**Sharing with clients?** Pages in this tab are written to the client ("you"), so you can send a link straight to them. Setup pages for your team are under [Clients and sales](/clients/portal-access).
</Info>

## Where it lives

The sidebar has **Welcome**, **Projects**, **Files**, **Meetings**, and **Messages**, plus a notification bell and a theme switch. The address is `/cp/@your-organization`, or your own domain on [Pro and above](/portal/roles-and-branding).

## What clients never see

Your internal Task names and assignees stay private. Progress appears only if you choose to share it, and Projects you hide do not appear at all. You control everything from [Client sharing](/clients/client-sharing).

## Preview as your client

Team members can choose **View as client** on a client or Project to see exactly what the client sees. It is read-only and lasts 30 minutes.
