Documents from your team show up under **Waiting on you**, on Project pages, and in **Files**.

## What you can do

| Document | Your action |
| --- | --- |
| **Quotation or proposal** | Accept or decline |
| **Contract or letter** | Sign with a typed name, or decline |
| **Handoff** | Confirm delivery |
| **Policy or certificate** | Read and download |

Every document can be downloaded as a **PDF**.

<Note>
**Viewer** contacts can read documents but cannot accept or sign. They can still **confirm delivery**.
</Note>
