## Create your organization

Founders sign up once. You create your account first, then your organization, and you become its **Admin**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up with your email, or continue with Google.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe your organization">
    Enter the organization name and choose a web address (slug) for it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer a few questions">
    Choose your organization type (Agency, Company, Freelance, Startup, or Other), team size, industry, country, how you plan to use Flotick, and how you heard about us. A website and referral code are optional.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Every signup question needs an answer. Nothing is pre-filled, which keeps your setup accurate.
</Note>

Every new organization starts on the **Free** plan. You can upgrade any time from [Billing](/admin/billing/upgrade-or-cancel).

## Sign in

Sign in with your email or username and password, or with Google. Forgot your password? Choose **Forgot password** and follow the email link.

| Security check | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **New device** | You may be asked for a verification code sent to your email. |
| **Two-factor authentication** | If you turned it on, you enter a code after your password. |
| **Repeated failures** | The account locks for a while to stop guessing. |
| **Email verification** | New accounts confirm their email address. |

You can keep several accounts signed in on one browser and switch between them from the account switcher.
