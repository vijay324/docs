<Badge color="green">All plans</Badge>

An Admin or Manager invites you by email. Open the link, set a password, and you are in.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the invitation email">
    Click the link in the email. It works for **72 hours**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your password">
    Choose a strong password. You are added to the organization with the role you were invited to (Manager or Member).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish onboarding">
    Complete your profile and preferences. See [Onboarding](/get-started/onboarding).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
If the link has expired, ask the person who invited you to resend it. Invitations can be resent or canceled at any time.
</Warning>

## For Admins and Managers: sending invites

Open **Members** and choose **Invite**. Pick a role, and the person receives an email.

- **Resend or cancel** any pending invite from the Members page.
- **Import many people** at once from a CSV or XLSX file.
- Each invite needs a **free seat**. Free has 5 seats, Starter 10, Pro 30, and Max 75. See [Seat management](/admin/billing/seat-management).
