## The sidebar

The sidebar groups the app by what you are doing. Items you cannot access are hidden, and items on a higher plan show a plan badge.

| Group | What is inside |
| --- | --- |
| **Work** | Inbox, Tasks, Sprints, Projects, Analytics |
| **Clients** | Leads, Messages, Clients, Meetings, Documents |
| **Team** | Attendance, Leave, My pay, Payroll, Members |
| **Files** | Files, Project keys |
| **Footer** | Settings, Public page, Refer and earn, Trash |

Some items appear only for Admins and Managers, such as Members and Payroll. **Attendance** and **Leave** are hidden for members whose job type is Contract.

### Make it yours

- Collapse the sidebar with `[`.
- Reorder or hide sections in [Preferences](/account/preferences).
- Use the **project scope chip** to view everything, or focus the whole app on one Project.
- Use the **organization menu** to switch organizations, invite members, upgrade, or sign out.

## Quick Add

Press **+** in the sidebar header to create a Task fast. Choose **Individual task** for personal work, or **Sprint task** to add it to a Sprint. You need permission to create Tasks.

## Search and command palette

Press `Ctrl/⌘ K` or `/` to open search. It finds Projects, Sprints, Tasks, members, and pages, and can run commands. Results respect your permissions and private Projects.

<Tip>
The [Personal board](/features/personal-board) has no sidebar link. Open it with search or the `G` then `B` shortcut.
</Tip>

## Help menu and Orbit

The bottom bar has two buttons. The **Flotick logo** opens the help menu: Docs, Contact us, Support and feature requests, Changelog, keyboard shortcuts, and the theme switch. The **Orbit button** opens the [AI assistant](/features/orbit), and the history button shows recent chats. On mobile, these are a floating button.
