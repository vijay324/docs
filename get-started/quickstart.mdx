This guide takes you through the setup that matters most. You can do everything else later.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your organization" icon="building-2">
    [Sign up](/get-started/sign-up), then give your organization a name and a web address. You become the Admin.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your first Project" icon="folder-kanban">
    Onboarding offers to create one for you. Add a name, optionally pick a client, and set dates. See [Projects](/features/projects).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a Task" icon="list-checks">
    Press **+** in the sidebar header, choose **Individual task**, and give it a title. See [Tasks](/features/tasks).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your team" icon="user-plus">
    Open **Members**, choose **Invite**, and pick a role. Each invite uses one seat. See [Members and roles](/admin/members-and-roles).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your first client" icon="briefcase">
    Open **Clients** and add a name, email, and mobile number. Link them to the Project. See [Clients](/clients/clients).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Optional, but worth doing

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Capture leads" icon="funnel" href="/clients/lead-forms">
    Embed a form on your website. Every enquiry becomes a lead.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Add your logo and details" icon="badge-check" href="/documents/business-profile">
    Fill in your business profile once. Quotes and contracts use it automatically.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Invite your client to the portal" icon="layout-template" href="/clients/portal-access">
    Give clients a place to see progress, approve work, and message you.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Try Orbit" icon="sparkles" href="/features/orbit">
    Ask a question about your work. Orbit answers with sources.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Setup checklist

New organizations show a **Complete setup x/4** badge at the bottom of the sidebar. It opens once on its own and tracks the steps above: create a Project, add a Task, invite a teammate, and add a client. Capturing leads and adding your logo are optional and can be skipped.

<Tip>
Start small. Five to ten people on one real Project teaches you more than a perfect setup nobody uses.
</Tip>
