The first time you sign in, Flotick walks you through a quick setup. Most steps can be skipped and finished later.

## The steps

| Step | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| **Welcome** | See what Flotick offers. |
| **Profile** | Add your photo, name, and job details. |
| **Organization** | Confirm your organization details. |
| **First Project** | Admins can create a Project, and optionally attach a client. |
| **Updates** | Choose how you want to hear about activity. |
| **Theme** | Pick light, dark, or system. |
| **Done** | Land in your [Inbox](/features/inbox). |

Admins follow the full path. Managers and Members complete their profile and preferences.

## Organization setup checklist

After onboarding, Admins of newer organizations see **Complete setup x/4** at the bottom of the sidebar. It opens once on its own.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a Project">
    Group your first piece of delivery. See [Projects](/features/projects).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a Task">
    Capture the first thing to do. See [Tasks](/features/tasks).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite a teammate">
    Bring someone in. See [Members and roles](/admin/members-and-roles).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a client">
    Link your first client. See [Clients](/clients/clients).
  </Step>
</Steps>

Two optional steps, **capture leads** and **add your logo**, can be skipped.

<Tip>
You can change your profile and theme later in [Preferences](/account/preferences) and [Profile](/account/profile).
</Tip>
