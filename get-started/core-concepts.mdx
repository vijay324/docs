## Organization

An **organization** is your company, agency, or freelance business inside Flotick. It owns the members, clients, Projects, documents, files, and billing. Each organization has its own web address (called a slug). Every new organization starts on the Free plan.

## Roles

Every member has one of three roles. Roles set the defaults for what each person can see and do.

| Role | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Admin** | The owner. Always has full access, and is the only role that can change billing, delete the organization, or transfer ownership. |
| **Manager** | Runs delivery and people. Full access to most areas, within limits an Admin sets. |
| **Member** | Does the work. Can view, create, and edit their own work in most areas. |

On [Pro and above](/admin/billing/plans), Admins and Managers can fine-tune one person's access. See [Member permissions](/admin/member-permissions).

## How clients fit in

A lead becomes a **client**. A client is linked to Projects, meetings, documents, and files. Clients get their own **Client Portal** where they follow progress, approve work, and talk to your team. They never see your internal Tasks.

## Short IDs

Projects and Tasks get readable IDs built from a short code for the Project. For example, `PAY-P-001` is a Project and `PAY-T-142` is a Task. Use them in chats and searches.

## Plans

Flotick has four plans: **Free**, **Starter**, **Pro**, and **Max**. Plans set limits (members, Projects, storage, AI credits) and unlock features. A badge such as <Badge color="blue">Starter+</Badge> means that plan and above. See [Plans](/admin/billing/plans).

## Orbit

**Orbit** is Flotick's AI assistant. It answers questions about your work using only what you are allowed to see, and can prepare changes for you to approve. See [Orbit](/features/orbit).

<Note>
Three things decide what you see: your role, your plan, and your access to the specific Project or record. If something is missing, check each one.
</Note>
