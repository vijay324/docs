Open **Settings → Personal → Preferences**. Changes apply instantly and follow you to other devices.

## Options

- **Font size**
- **Sidebar**: order, visibility, and badges
- **Themes**: built-in and **custom** themes
- **Sidebar theme**: a separate theme just for the sidebar
- **Import and export** themes to share or back up

<Tip>
Press `[` to collapse the sidebar quickly, and use the theme switch in the help menu to flip between light and dark.
</Tip>
