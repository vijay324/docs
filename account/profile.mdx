Open **Settings → Personal → Profile**.

## What you can set

- **Photo**, **name**, and **contact** details
- **Job title** and **department**
- **Google account connection**, which powers [Google Calendar sync](/clients/meetings#google-calendar-sync)
- **Consent** to appear on your organization's [public business page](/grow/business-page)

Flotick reminds you until required fields are filled in.
