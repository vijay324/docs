Open **Settings → Personal → Security**.

## Password

**Change password** signs you out of every other device.

## Sessions

- **Allow or block multiple sessions** at once.
- See a **live list** of where you are signed in.

## Two-factor authentication (2FA)

Add a second step to your sign-in.

| Method | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| **Authenticator app** | Scan a QR code, then enter a code from your app. You get **10 backup codes**. |
| **Email code** | A code is sent to your email. |

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a method under Security" />
  <Step title="Scan the QR code, or enable email codes" />
  <Step title="Save your backup codes">
    Store them somewhere safe. Each works once if you lose your device.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Delete your account

You can delete your account from this page.

<Warning>
Keep your backup codes private. Anyone with a backup code can sign in as you.
</Warning>
