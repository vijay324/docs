Share a document with one link. Your client opens it in a browser, with no account, and responds.

## How clients respond

| Document type | What the client does |
| --- | --- |
| **Quotations and proposals** | **Accept** |
| **Contracts and letters** | **Sign** with a typed name |
| **Handoffs** | **Confirm** delivery |
| **Policies and certificates** | View and download only |

The PDF shows your organization's [verified tier](/grow/verified-tick). Old `/q/` links still work and redirect.

## Send a document

<Steps>
  <Step title="Finish the document">
    Fill in every required blank.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Share and copy the link">
    Send it by email, chat, or WhatsApp.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch for the response">
    You are notified when it is accepted, signed, or declined.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Clients with [portal access](/clients/portal-access) also see the document listed in their portal, under **Waiting on you**.

## Limits

The **first share each month** of a document counts toward the monthly limit: **20 on Free**, unlimited on Starter and above.
