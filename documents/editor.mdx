The editor feels like a page, not a form. You write, fill in the blanks, pick a design, and send.

## What you can do

- **Autosave** with version checks, so you never lose changes or overwrite a teammate.
- **Fill in panel** for blanks and auto-filled client details such as `{{client.name}}`.
- **Design picker**. Locked designs preview under a **PREVIEW** stamp.
- **Payment schedules** inside quotes.
- **Download PDF** and **duplicate**.

## AI Builder

The AI Builder drafts quotations and contracts from the organization knowledge you saved in [Document studio](/documents/document-studio). It uses [AI credits](/features/orbit#credits). Review everything before you send.

## Locked documents

Once a document is **accepted, signed, or expired**, it is locked. To change it, **duplicate** it and edit the copy.

<Warning>
A document cannot be shared while required blanks are empty. Open **Fill in** to see what is missing.
</Warning>
