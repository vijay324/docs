<Badge color="green">All plans</Badge>

When a client accepts your quotation, you can start the work immediately. **Start the work** builds the Project for you.

## What it creates

- A new **Project**
- **Backlog Tasks** to plan from
- **Milestones** your client can follow

## Use it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the accepted quotation" />
  <Step title="Choose Start the work">
    Review what will be created.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Your new Project opens, ready to plan.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Quote kickoff needs room under your Project limit. See [Projects](/features/projects#limits-and-downgrades).
</Note>
