Enter your details one time in **Settings → Workspace → Business profile**. Every document pulls from here, so nothing is retyped.

## What to fill in

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Logo** | Starter and above |
| **Address, email, phone, website** | Shown on documents |
| **Tax ID and its label** | GSTIN, VAT, TIN, EIN, ABN, or TRN |
| **Bank details** | Stored encrypted and masked |
| **Number prefixes** | For document numbering |
| **Default currency** | Used on new documents |
| **Payment due days** | Default terms |
| **Default notes and terms** | Reused on every document |

Everyone can read the profile, but only Admins and Managers edit it.

<Note>
Members who do not have access to payments information see placeholders in templates instead of your bank details.
</Note>
