Make Orbit sound like your team. Open **Settings → Features → Orbit AI**.

## What you can set

- **Response tone**: **Concise**, **Warm**, or **Formal**
- **Custom terminology** and a **glossary** of your own words
- **Known team members** and **active Projects**
- **Learned guidance**: review and approve what Orbit picked up
- **Feedback** on answers
- **Credit usage** for the last 30 days

Personal preferences are **private to each person**.
