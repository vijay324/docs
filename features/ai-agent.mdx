Orbit Agent turns an instruction into a **plan of changes**. You review it, confirm it, and only then does anything happen.

## What it can do

- Create or update **leads**, log activity, and **convert leads** to clients
- Create **clients** and link **Projects** to clients
- Schedule **meetings** (invites are disclosed to you first)
- Create, assign, or comment on **Tasks**
- Create **Projects** and **Sprints**

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Orbit and switch to agent mode">
    Type what you want, for example "Add a lead for Maya at Northwind and schedule a call on Friday".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the plan">
    Orbit lists every change it will make.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Approve the plan, and Orbit carries it out. Admins can **undo** changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Safety limits

- Each plan costs **5 credits**.
- A plan **expires after 10 minutes** if you do not confirm.
- Up to **50 actions per hour** per organization.
- Destructive actions need a separate approval.

<Note>
Orbit Agent only does what your own permissions allow.
</Note>
