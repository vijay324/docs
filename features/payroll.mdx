Payroll is built India-first. It covers salary structures, monthly pay runs, **PF, ESI, professional tax, and TDS**, loans, claims, adjustments, payslips, and full-and-final settlement.

## For payroll managers

| Section | What is inside |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | Summary of the current cycle |
| **Pay cycle** | Pay runs, payslips, adjustments, full and final |
| **People** | Employees and salary structures |
| **Requests** | Claims, loans and advances, tax declarations |
| **My pay** | Your own payslips |
| **Setup** | Payroll configuration |

## A pay run

Runs move through **Draft → Pending approval → Approved → Finalized → Paid**. You can turn on **two-person approval**, **reopen** a run, **hold** one employee, and **record manual salary payments** (single or bulk).

Outputs include a **bank file**, a **register**, a **statutory summary**, a **payslip ZIP**, and **emailed payslips**. Choose from **11 payslip designs** (1 Free, 6 Starter, 4 Pro).

## For members: My pay

Members see **My pay** in the sidebar: their **payslips, payments, and salary**, plus **Requests** for claims and loans. Members only ever see their own pay.

## Good to know

- Admins run payroll. Managers have no payroll access unless granted. See [Member permissions](/admin/member-permissions).
- Statutory deductions apply to **INR** only, and runs are **monthly**.
- After a downgrade, **past payslips stay readable**.
