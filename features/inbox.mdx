Your Inbox is the page you land on after signing in. It brings together what happened while you were away and what you should do next.

## What you will find

- **Notifications** about assignments, comments, @mentions, reactions, approvals, and client activity.
- **Your personal Tasks**, so you can act without leaving the page.
- **Task details** when you open a sprint-task notification: acceptance criteria, subtasks, and comments, right in the Inbox.

Unread items are marked. Mark one as read, or mark everything at once. New items arrive live, so you never need to refresh.

## Today brief

<Badge color="blue">New</Badge>

At the top of the Inbox, **Today brief** shows your plan for the day:

| Section | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Overdue** | Tasks past their due date |
| **Due today** | Tasks due today |
| **Follow-ups** | Lead follow-ups scheduled for today |
| **Meetings** | Meetings happening today |
| **Waiting on me** | Reviews, testing, leave approvals, payment proofs, and client change requests |

Each count opens a filtered list. You only see sections you have access to.

An optional **AI summary** turns the brief into a sentence or two. It uses [AI credits](/features/orbit#credits) and falls back to a plain summary when AI is unavailable.

<Tip>
Start your day with the brief, clear "Waiting on me" first, then work down your overdue Tasks.
</Tip>
