Attendance records when your team works. Members check in and out themselves, and Managers see the whole picture.

## Check in and out

Open **Attendance** and choose **Check in**. A live **work timer** runs until you check out. When you check out, add a **work description**. Use **Enhance with AI** to polish it. See [AI writing helpers](/features/ai-writing).

Choose your **work type**: **Office**, **Remote**, or **Hybrid**.

## Views

| Tab | Who sees it |
| --- | --- |
| **Table** | Everyone, for their own records |
| **Calendar** | Everyone |
| **Team** | Admins and Managers (Starter+) |

Switch between **organization** and **mine** scopes.

## Smart handling

- **Late arrival** and **overtime** (over 8 hours) are detected automatically.
- **Check-outs after midnight** are handled correctly.
- **Corrections**: request a fix (regularization) and a Manager approves it.
- **Backfill**: Admins can fill in forgotten days.
- **Export** the data (Starter+).

Approved [leave](/features/leave) is written into attendance automatically.

<Note>
Attendance is hidden for members whose job type is **Contract**.
</Note>
