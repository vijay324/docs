The Personal board answers one question: **what is on my plate?** It gathers your Tasks from every Project into a single board, so you do not have to hop between Projects.

## What appears on it

- **Sprint tasks** where you hold any role: assignee, reviewer, tester, or reporter.
- **Individual tasks** you created.

Move a card to update its status. Changes follow the same workflow rules as everywhere else.

## Open it

The board lives at `/board` and has no sidebar link. Use search (`Ctrl/⌘ K`) or press `G` then `B`.

<Tip>
Pair it with the [Inbox](/features/inbox) Today brief: the brief tells you what needs attention, and the board shows everything you are carrying.
</Tip>
