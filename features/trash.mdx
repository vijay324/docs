When you delete something in Flotick, it moves to **Trash** first. You have **30 days** to restore it before it is removed permanently.

## Where to find it

Open **Trash** in the sidebar footer (`/trash`).

| Tab | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Tasks** | Deleted Tasks |
| **Sprints** | Deleted Sprints |
| **Projects** | Deleted Projects |
| **Members** | Deleted members |
| **Files** | Deleted files |
| **Sales** | Leads and other CRM items |
| **More** | Everything else |

## Restore or remove

- **Restore** puts an item back. Restoring a Project also brings back its Sprints, Tasks, and files.
- **Delete permanently** removes it right away and cannot be undone.

Who can restore or permanently delete depends on the type of item.

<Note>
Two areas keep their own trash: **Tasks** (`/tasks/trash`) and **Documents** (`/documents/trash`). Deleted meetings send their notes and files to Trash too.
</Note>
