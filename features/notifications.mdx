Flotick tells you when something needs your attention. Notifications arrive live, so you do not have to refresh.

## What triggers a notification

- A Task is **assigned** to you
- Someone **comments**, **@mentions** you, or reacts to your comment
- A **workflow decision** or approval is made
- A **client** acts in the portal (approves, asks, uploads, or sends a proof)
- A **billing** event affects your organization

Some events also send an **email**.

## Where you see them

| Place | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| **Bell in the header** | Opens a dropdown of recent notifications with an unread count. |
| **Inbox** | The full list, with actions and task previews. See [Inbox](/features/inbox). |
| **Browser notifications** | Flotick asks once if it may show alerts, even when the tab is in the background. |

Use **Mark as read** on one item, or mark them all in one click.

## Live updates

When a teammate changes a Task, moves a board card, posts a comment, or finishes an Orbit action, your open lists and details refresh on their own.

If a view looks out of date:

1. Open the bell and check that it shows **Live**.
2. Make sure your device is online. Click the offline indicator to retry.
3. Refresh the page if the view is still stale.

<Note>
Saved changes are always checked by the server. Live updates only keep your screen current.
</Note>
