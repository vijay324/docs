Analytics turns your Task and Sprint activity into clear answers: Are we on time? Who is overloaded? Is delivery getting faster?

## Views

| View | For |
| --- | --- |
| **Organization** | Admins and Managers, the big picture |
| **Work details** | Delivery by Project and Sprint |
| **People details** | Workload and contribution |
| **Compare** | Side-by-side periods |
| **Me** | Your own numbers, for every member |

Choose a **time range** and scope by Project, Sprint, or member. Drill into any chart, and save your favorite setup as a **saved view**.

## What you can measure

Insights banner, period comparisons, leaderboards, activity heatmap, **cycle time**, **on-time delivery**, **due-date risk**, **workload balance**, **stage latency**, **rework**, and **time on task**. Export to **CSV, XLSX, or PDF**.
