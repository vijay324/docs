Files collects everything your team uploads in one searchable place, and lets you share the right files with your client.

## Browse

Open **Files**. Tabs are **All files**, **Projects**, **Sprints**, **Tasks**, and **Unassigned**. Filter by tag, switch between grid and list, and watch the storage meter.

## What you can do

- **Upload** by drag and drop. Uploads run in the background, so keep working.
- **Download** and **tag** files.
- **Share a Project file with the client** so it appears in their portal.
- **Request client approval** of a file. See [Approvals](/portal/approvals).
- **Version history** keeps earlier versions of a file <Badge color="blue">Starter+</Badge>.
- **Delete** to [Trash](/features/trash) and restore within 30 days.

Individual Tasks cannot have attachments. Members can view files and can be allowed to upload.
