Orbit is Flotick's AI assistant. Ask in plain language, and Orbit answers from your own data, with **cited sources**. It only uses what **you** are allowed to see.

## Ask Orbit

Open the **Orbit button** in the bottom bar, open `/inbox/orbit`, or use the side sheet. Try questions such as:

- "What is overdue on the Acme Project?"
- "Which leads need a follow-up this week?"
- "Summarize yesterday's client meeting."

Orbit covers **work, CRM, meetings, and documents**. Your conversation history is saved.

## Choose a model

| Model | Best for | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Auto** | Let Orbit decide (Free uses Auto) | 1 credit |
| **Orbit** | Fast answers | 1 credit |
| **Orbit Pro** | Harder questions | 3 credits |

Picking the model yourself needs **Starter or above**.
