AI writing helpers do the first draft. You stay in charge: every result is a **suggestion you must accept**.

## Where they appear

| Where | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Task and Sprint creation** | Drafts content from your title and description |
| **Attendance check-out** | **Enhance with AI** polishes your work notes |
| **Documents** | The [AI Builder](/documents/editor#ai-builder) drafts quotes and contracts |
| **Lead replies** | **Draft reply** suggests an email. See [Lead autopilot](/clients/lead-autopilot) |
| **Today brief** | An optional AI summary of your day |

## Credits

Writing costs **2 credits**, and rewriting costs **1**. See [Orbit credits](/features/orbit#credits).

<Tip>
If AI is unavailable, everything still works by hand. Nothing is blocked behind it.
</Tip>
