Stay informed without opening Analytics. Scheduled reports land in your inbox.

## Set one up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Features → Scheduled reports" />
  <Step title="Choose a schedule">
    **Weekly** or **monthly**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a format">
    **PDF**, **CSV**, or **XLSX**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send a sample">
    Optionally send one now to check how it looks.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Reports go to **Admins and Managers**.
