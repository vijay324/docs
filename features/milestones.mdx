Milestones turn a long Project into a few clear stages. Clients see them as a **roadmap** in their portal, so they always know where things stand.

## What you can do

- Add up to **30 milestones** per Project, each with dates and a status.
- **Drag to reorder** them.
- **Hide** a milestone from the client.
- **Link Tasks and files** so progress is based on real work.
- Change a date and add a **reason**. The client sees "moved from" with your explanation.
- **Request client approval** on a milestone. See [Approvals](/portal/approvals).

An accepted quote can create milestones for you. See [Quote kickoff](/documents/quote-kickoff).

## Add a milestone

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Project and choose Milestones" />
  <Step title="Add a title, dates, and status">
    Use client-friendly names, such as "Design approved".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link Tasks and files">
    Progress updates as linked Tasks are completed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Keep milestones to outcomes your client cares about. Leave internal steps as Tasks.
</Tip>
