Leave handles requests, balances, and approvals without spreadsheets.

## Leave types

**Privilege**, **Sick**, **Casual**, **Earned**, **Maternity**, **Paternity**, **Comp-off**, and **Loss of pay**. Half days count as **0.5**.

## Request leave

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Leave and choose a request">
    Pick the type, dates, and whether it is a half day.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Your Manager is notified.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track it">
    See the status under **My requests**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tabs

| Tab | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| **My requests** | Your own requests and balances |
| **Pending** | Requests waiting for approval (Managers) |
| **Team** | Who is off |
| **Upcoming** | Approved leave ahead |
| **Comp-off** | Time off earned for extra work |
| **Accrual** | How balances grow |

## What needs Starter+

**Comp-off**, **encashment**, **accrual logs**, the **team calendar**, and **upcoming leave** need Starter or above. Basic requesting and approving works on every plan.

Members can view and create requests. Managers approve. Approved leave is written into [attendance](/features/attendance). Leave is hidden for **Contract** members.
