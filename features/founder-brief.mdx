Start the week knowing where you stand. The Monday founder brief is a short email with your organization's key numbers.

## Set it up

Open **Settings → Workspace → General** and find **Monday founder brief**. Turn it on, and preview it up to **three times a day**.

## Optional AI opening

The brief can start with a short, AI-written paragraph. To keep it trustworthy, Flotick **drops the AI opening** if it mentions any number that is not found in your data.
