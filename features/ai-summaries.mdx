**AI Summary** writes the report so you do not have to. It reads your real data and explains it in plain language.

## What it can summarize

- **Weekly, monthly, and Sprint** summaries
- **Bottlenecks**
- **Workload**
- **Deadline risk**
- **Team health**

You will find it in the **Analytics hub** as the **executive brief**. Each report is saved in **history**, and you can give it a thumbs up or down so Flotick improves.

## Good to know

- Summaries use [AI credits](/features/orbit#credits).
- If AI is unavailable, you get a **plain summary** from the same data instead.
- Reports are suggestions. Check the numbers against [Analytics](/features/analytics) before you share them.
