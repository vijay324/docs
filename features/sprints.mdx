A Sprint is a short, focused round of work inside a Project. It gives your team a goal, a deadline, and a clear picture of progress.

## Create a Sprint

Open **Sprints** and choose **Create**, or add one from a Project. Set:

- **Project**, **title**, and **goal**
- **Dates** and **capacity**
- **Members** and attachments
- Tasks **carried over** from earlier Sprints

## Lifecycle

Sprints move through **Planning → Active → Review → Completed**. A Project can have **one active Sprint** at a time. Only Admins and Managers move a Sprint between stages.

## Inside a Sprint

| Tab | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | Goal, dates, progress, and Tasks |
| **Insights** | Commitment coaching and scope tracking |
| **Members** | Who is on the Sprint |
| **Files** | Sprint attachments |
| **Comments** | Team discussion |
| **Analytics** | Burndown and velocity |

Also built in: **daily standups**, a **retrospective** after completion, and **carrying unfinished Tasks** forward.

<Tip>
AI coaching in the Insights tab uses [AI credits](/features/orbit#credits).
</Tip>

Members can view Sprints by default and can be allowed to create and edit them.
