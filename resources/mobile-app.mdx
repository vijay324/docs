The Flotick mobile app puts your day-to-day work in your pocket.

## What is inside

Home, Inbox, Tasks, Projects, Sprints, Attendance (including corrections and team view), Leave (requests, comp-off, team, upcoming), CRM (leads, clients, meetings), Messages, Documents, Approvals, Team (invite and create), Search, and Profile.

## Good to know

- It uses the **same permissions and plan limits** as the web app.
- You sign in to **one organization** at a time.
- You can **sign in with Google**, but you need to **sign up on the web**.
- You can change your password, turn on two-factor sign-in, and manage your account in the app.
