---
title: "Glossary"
description: "Plain-language definitions of the terms used across Flotick."
icon: "book-open"
---

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Admin** | The owner of an organization, with full access. |
| **AI credits** | Monthly allowance used by Orbit and AI helpers. |
| **Approver** | A client contact who can accept, sign, and approve. |
| **Backlog** | Tasks planned but not yet in a Sprint. |
| **Client** | A customer you do work for, with a record linked to Projects and documents. |
| **Client Portal** | The branded space where clients follow progress and approve work. |
| **Comp-off** | Time off earned by working extra. |
| **Discovery form** | A questionnaire sent to a prospect from a lead. |
| **Epic** | A Task type that groups child Tasks. |
| **Individual task** | A private Task with no Sprint. |
| **Lead** | A potential client in your sales pipeline. |
| **Manager** | A role that runs delivery and people. |
| **Member** | A person in your organization. Also the default role. |
| **Milestone** | A client-friendly stage of a Project. |
| **Orbit** | Flotick's AI assistant. |
| **Orbit Agent** | Orbit's mode that plans changes for you to confirm. |
| **Organization** | Your company, agency, or freelance business in Flotick. |
| **Project** | All the work for one piece of delivery. |
| **Project keys** | An encrypted vault for `.env` settings. |
| **Seat** | One member slot on your plan. |
| **Slug** | The web address name of your organization. |
| **Sprint** | A time-boxed round of work inside a Project. |
| **Sprint task** | A Task that belongs to a Sprint. |
| **Today brief** | Your daily plan at the top of the Inbox. |
| **Verified tick** | A trust badge on your business page, portal, and documents. |
| **Viewer** | A client contact who can read but not decide. |
