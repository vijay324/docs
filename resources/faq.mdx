## Plans and billing

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which plan do I start on?">
    Every new organization starts on **Free**. Upgrade any time from **Settings → Workspace → Billing**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there a free trial?">
    **Pro** has a 7-day free trial, once per organization.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if I downgrade?">
    Nothing is deleted. Members and Projects over the new limit are locked, and features above the plan switch off. Upgrade, and they come back. See [Plan enforcement](/admin/billing/plan-enforcement).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where are the current prices?">
    At [flotick.com/pricing](https://flotick.com/pricing).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Access and people

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I see a page or button?">
    Check three things: your **role**, your **plan**, and your access to that **Project or record**. Ask an Admin to review your [permissions](/admin/member-permissions).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I invite someone?">
    You may be out of **seats**, or not an Admin or Manager. See [Seat management](/admin/billing/seat-management).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why don't I see Attendance or Leave?">
    They are hidden for members whose job type is **Contract**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I remove someone who left?">
    Use [Offboarding](/admin/offboarding) to deactivate them and hand over their work.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Work and clients

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is the difference between a Sprint task and an individual task?">
    Sprint tasks belong to a Sprint, have the full workflow, and support comments and files. Individual tasks are private to you and have a simple flow. See [Tasks](/features/tasks).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What do clients see in the portal?">
    Only what you share. Task names and assignees are never shown. See [Client sharing](/clients/client-sharing).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I preview what a client sees?">
    Use **View as client** on a client or Project. It is a read-only, 30-minute preview.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can a client sign a document without an account?">
    Yes. Shared documents open from a link, and clients accept or sign with a typed name. See [Sharing and signing](/documents/sharing-and-signing).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Orbit and AI

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does Orbit see everything?">
    No. Orbit only uses what **you** are allowed to see.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will AI send things without asking?">
    No. AI results are suggestions. Orbit Agent shows a plan and waits for your confirmation.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens when I run out of AI credits?">
    Features that use credits pause until the next month or an upgrade. Everything still works by hand. See [Orbit credits](/features/orbit#credits).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Data

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How long do deleted items stay in Trash?">
    30 days. See [Trash](/features/trash).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I export my data?">
    Yes. Admins can download a ZIP of all organization data. See [Data and privacy](/admin/data-and-privacy).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
