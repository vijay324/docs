Your clients trust you with their work. Here is how Flotick helps you protect it.

## Sign-in protection

- **Two-factor authentication** with an authenticator app or email code, plus backup codes
- **New-device verification** by email code
- **Account lockout** after repeated failed sign-ins
- **Session controls**: live session list and an option to block multiple sessions
- **Email verification** for new accounts

See [Security and 2FA](/account/security-and-2fa).

## Secrets and sensitive data

- [Project keys](/features/env-storage) and [Project credentials](/features/project-credentials) are **encrypted**, and credential reveals are **logged**.
- **Bank details** in your business profile are encrypted and masked.
- **Meeting notes** are stored encrypted.
- [Messages](/clients/messages) and attachments are **end-to-end encrypted**.

## Access control

- **Roles and permissions** limit what each person sees. See [Member permissions](/admin/member-permissions).
- **Private Projects** are limited to their members.
- **Orbit** only uses what you are allowed to see.
- Clients only see what you share, and **never** your Task names or assignees.

## Your data

- **Trash** keeps deleted items for 30 days.
- Admins can **export** all organization data, with secrets removed.
- The owner can **request deletion**, with 30 days to restore.

See [Data and privacy](/admin/data-and-privacy).

## Report a concern

Email [support@flotick.com](mailto:support@flotick.com) with **Security** in the subject. Never send passwords, codes, or keys.
