Open **Support** from the help menu or the Help page (`/support`). You can also email [support@flotick.com](mailto:support@flotick.com).

## Send a request

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a type">
    **Get help**, **Report a bug**, or **Request a feature**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the details">
    Pick a category and urgency, write a short summary, and describe what happened. Attach up to **5 screenshots** (10 MB each).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit and follow along">
    You get a ticket number such as `SR-1042`, and replies appear in the thread.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How fast we reply

| Urgency | Target |
| --- | --- |
| **Blocking my work** | Within 4 business hours |
| **Important** | Within 1 business day |
| **Minor** | Within 3 business days |
| **Feature ideas** | Reviewed weekly |

## Ticket statuses

**Open**, **In progress**, **Waiting for you**, and **Resolved**.

## Tips for a faster answer

- Include the **page** and **what you expected**.
- Add a **screenshot** with no passwords or private data.
- For security concerns, email support with **Security** in the subject. Never send passwords, codes, or keys.

<Note>
Attachments count toward your storage and need permission to upload files.
</Note>
