Know someone who needs Flotick? Share your link and earn when they become a paying customer.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Refer and earn in the sidebar footer">
    Copy your referral link (`/r/your-code`). The **kit** page has ready-made material to share.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share it">
    People who sign up with your link get a **discount or extra trial days**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Earn">
    You earn a share of what referred customers pay.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Getting paid

Payouts are **manual**, after PAN and one-time-code checks. Or you can turn your balance into **Flotick credit**.

## Tiers

Your referral tier grows with results: **Starter**, **Partner**, and **Ambassador**. These tiers are separate from product plans.

<Note>
Referral terms, rates, and payout rules can change. Check the in-app **Refer and earn** page for the latest.
</Note>
