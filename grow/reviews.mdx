Social proof sells. Flotick makes it easy to ask for a review after a Project is done and to display it.

## Collect reviews

1. Open your [business page](/grow/business-page) and invite a client to review a **completed Project**.
2. The client gets a **single-use link** that works for **14 days**.
3. Once submitted, you can **reply**, **hide**, or **report** the review.

## Show them on your website

Embed a **review badge** or a **widget** on any page. On Starter and above you can customize the look, use the AI install prompt, and collect reviews directly on your site.
