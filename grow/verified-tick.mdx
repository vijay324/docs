The verified tick tells clients and visitors that your business is real. It appears on your business page, client portal, and documents once checks pass.

## The three tiers

| Tier | Color | Requirements |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Starter** | Gray | Verified email |
| **Pro** | Blue | Everything above, plus a **verified domain** and **approved business documents** reviewed by the Flotick team |
| **Max** | Gold | Everything above, plus **10 completed Projects** and **5 published reviews** |

Manage verification in **Settings → Workspace → Domain and verification**.

## Earned badges

Any organization can earn these badges, on any plan:

- **Top Rated**
- **Fast Responder**
- **50+ Projects Delivered**
- **Years on Flotick**
