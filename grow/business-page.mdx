Your business page is a public profile at `/@your-handle`, and it also appears in the Flotick business directory. It is a quick way to look credible and collect enquiries.

## What goes on the page

Headline, type, location, year started, languages, **services** (you can import them from quotations), **portfolio** from completed Projects, **reviews**, team members who agree to appear, and a **Request a quote** form that creates a [lead](/clients/leads).

## Publish it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Public page in the sidebar footer" />
  <Step title="Fill in the sections">
    Use the **page-strength checklist** to see what is missing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a handle and publish">
    Save as a draft first, and publish when you are happy.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After publishing, you get **30-day stats** on visits and enquiries.
