When someone leaves, **Deactivate and hand over** shuts off their access immediately and walks you through everything that needs a new owner.

## What the wizard covers

| Area | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| **Tasks** | Reassign, with suggested new owners |
| **Leads** | Move to a teammate |
| **Meetings** | Reassign or remove |
| **Project memberships** | Update who is on each Project |
| **Credentials** | See which logins to rotate |
| **Payroll** | Review before the final settlement |

## Start it

Open **Members**, choose the person, and select **Deactivate and hand over**. Deleting a member who still owns open work opens the same wizard.

<Note>
You cannot offboard the Admin or yourself.
</Note>
