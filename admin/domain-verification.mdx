Open **Settings → Workspace → Domain and verification**.

## Custom portal domain

Put the [Client Portal](/portal/overview) on an address like `client.yourcompany.com`.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your subdomain" />
  <Step title="Add the DNS records Flotick shows you">
    Add them at your domain provider.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check">
    Flotick checks in the background. Choose **Check now** to try right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can **Remove** the domain at any time. The standard portal address keeps working. If you **downgrade**, the custom domain switches off.
