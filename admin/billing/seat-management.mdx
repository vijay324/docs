A **seat** is one member of your organization.

| | Free | Starter | Pro | Max |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Seats | 5 | 10 | 30 | 75 |

## Rules

- Every **invite** needs a free seat.
- Free a seat by **deactivating** or deleting a member. See [Offboarding](/admin/offboarding).
- **Pending invites** can be canceled to free their seat.
- Need more room? [Upgrade your plan](/admin/billing/upgrade-or-cancel).

## After a downgrade

If you have more members than your new plan allows, the **newest members over the limit are locked out** until you upgrade. The **owner is never locked**. Nothing is deleted.
