Open **Settings → Workspace → Billing**. Payments are handled securely by **Polar**.

## See where you stand

The page shows your current plan, its **status**, your **usage**, and your **billing history**.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Active** | Paid and running |
| **Trialing** | In a free trial |
| **Canceling** | Ends at the close of the billing period |
| **Past due** | A payment failed |
| **Ended** | The plan has ended |
| **Complimentary** | A plan provided by Flotick |
| **Free** | The default plan |

## Upgrade

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a plan on Billing, or visit /pricing" />
  <Step title="Pick monthly or yearly" />
  <Step title="Check out">
    Upgrading to a higher plan is **prorated**, so you only pay the difference. You return to a confirmation page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Pro** has a **7-day free trial**, once per organization.

## Manage payment

Use the **payment portal** link to update your card or download invoices.

## Cancel

Choose **Cancel** in the app and tell us why. Your plan stays active until the end of the period. See [Plan enforcement](/admin/billing/plan-enforcement) for what changes afterwards.

<Note>
Only Admins can change plans. Prices and terms are listed at [flotick.com/pricing](https://flotick.com/pricing).
</Note>
