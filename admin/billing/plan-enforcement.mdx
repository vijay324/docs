title: "Plan enforcement and downgrades"
description: "What happens when you reach a limit or move to a lower plan. Nothing is ever deleted."

---

Plans set limits. When you downgrade or go over, Flotick **limits access, never deletes**. Upgrade, and everything comes back.

## What happens on a downgrade

| Item | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Members over the limit** | The newest are locked out. The owner is never locked. |
| **Projects over the limit** | They become **read-only**. |
| **Features above your plan** | Switch off immediately. |
| **Custom portal domain** | Switches off. |
| **Past payslips** | Stay readable. |
| **Saved permission overrides** | Kept, but not used. |

## Restricted resources

A **Restricted resources** panel lists exactly what is locked, so you know what to upgrade for.

## At a limit

When you reach a limit (for example, 5 active Projects on Free), upgrade or free up room before adding more. Existing work is unaffected.
