You own your data. Open **Settings → Workspace → General → Data and privacy**.

## Export your data

An Admin can download a **ZIP of all organization data**. Secrets are removed from the export. The download link **expires after 72 hours**.

## Delete your organization

The **owner** can request deletion.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request deletion">
    Give a reason and type the organization name to confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Flotick team approves">
    You are notified when the request is approved.
  </Step>
  <Step title="30 days to change your mind">
    You can **restore** during this window. After it, data is permanently removed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Deletion cannot be undone after the 30 days. Export first if you may need your data.
</Warning>
