The policy page tells Flotick how your organization works, so attendance and leave follow your rules. Open **Settings → Features → Attendance and leave**.

## Attendance rules

- **Working hours** and **default work type**
- **Employment type**
- **Weekend rules**
- **Late-arrival window**
- **Past-date entries**: whether people can record time for earlier days
- **Overtime**
- **Maximum consecutive days** in a row

## Leave rules

- **Yearly allowance** for each leave type
- **Accrual**: how balances build up over the year

<Tip>
Set the policy before inviting your whole team, so everyone starts with the right balances.
</Tip>
