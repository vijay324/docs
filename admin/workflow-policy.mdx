Every team works a little differently. Workflow lets you shape Tasks and Sprints to fit.

## What you can customize

- **Task statuses** and the **allowed moves** between them
- **Priorities**
- **Sprint length** and **story points**
- **Departments**, on their own tab. See [Departments](/admin/departments)

Open **Settings → Features → Workflow**.

<Warning>
Changes apply to **future work only**. Existing Tasks keep what they have.
</Warning>

On Free, the standard workflow is used.
