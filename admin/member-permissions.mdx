Sometimes a Member needs a bit more access, or less. Permissions let you tune it person by person.

## How it works

Open a member and choose the **Permissions** tab. For each area, allow or deny **view**, **create**, **edit**, and **delete**. The **role's ceiling** always applies, so you cannot grant more than the role allows.

- **Admins** can edit anyone except other Admins.
- **Managers** can edit Members only, and cannot grant access they do not have.

## Examples

- Let a Member **create and edit Projects**.
- Let a Member **upload Files**.
- Let a Member **view Project keys**.
- Let a Manager **see Payroll**.

## On Free and Starter

Everyone gets their role's defaults. Saved overrides are kept but not used until you upgrade to Pro.

<Note>
Your plan, your membership status, and access to the specific Project are checked separately from permissions.
</Note>
