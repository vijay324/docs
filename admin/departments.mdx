Departments group your team, such as Design, Engineering, or Sales. They show up in member profiles, onboarding, and the member list.

## Free and paid

**Free** uses a built-in default tree. On **Starter and above**, you can build your own.

## Customize

Open **Settings → Features → Workflow → Departments**. Departments have **two levels**: a department, and sub-departments inside it. Add, rename, or remove them to match your organization.
