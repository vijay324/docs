The Clients list is your client directory. Each client links to everything you do for them, so you never hunt for context.

## Add a client

Open **Clients** and choose **Add**. Or [convert a lead](/clients/leads) when you win the deal.

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | Required |
| **Email and mobile** | Required when you add a client directly |
| **GSTIN** | Optional, 15 characters |
| **Invoice currency** | INR, USD, EUR, GBP, AUD, CAD, AED, or SGD |

## The client page

Open a client to see everything in one place. Actions: **New project**, **Message**, **Edit**, **Delete**.

| Tab | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Client Portal** | Portal access and requests. See [Portal access](/clients/portal-access) |
| **Files** | Files shared with this client |
| **Projects** | Their Projects |
| **Meetings** | Past and upcoming meetings |
| **Activity** | What they did in the portal |

Search clients by name, email, phone, or GSTIN, and export the list as CSV or JSON.

## Limits

| | Free | Starter | Pro | Max |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Converting a lead counts toward the limit. The client list is shared across the whole organization.
