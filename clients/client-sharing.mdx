Each Project has a **Client** tab. It is one panel that controls what the client sees in their portal.

## What you can control

- **Portal access** status for the client
- **Shared files** and **shared credentials**
- **Shared meeting notes**
- **Progress sharing** (on or off)
- A **client-facing health note**
- **Who from your team is shown**, with role labels and a main contact
- **Hide from portal**, which removes the Project completely (Admins and Managers only)

The tab also shows context cards: what you are **waiting on the client** for, their requests, the next meeting, and recent activity. It includes the client's timeline view and a feedback card.

## View as client

Choose **View as client** to open a **read-only, 30-minute preview** of the Project's portal page. A banner shows it is a preview. It does not count as a client visit and does not mark anything as seen.

<Tip>
Preview before you publish an update or share a file. It takes five seconds and avoids surprises.
</Tip>

<Warning>
Task names and assignees are never shown to clients. Progress appears only if you choose to share it.
</Warning>
