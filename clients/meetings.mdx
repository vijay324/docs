Meetings schedules calls with clients and your team, sends calendar invites, and keeps notes next to the client record.

## Schedule a meeting

Open **Meetings** and choose **New**. Set the title, client, Project, date, time, duration, and attendee emails. Add a **Google Meet** link or paste your own. Attendees get an email invite with a calendar file. Pick a **reminder**.

Views: **Agenda**, **Week**, and **Month**.

## Notes and files

- **Notes** up to 10,000 characters, stored privately and encrypted.
- **Transcripts and documents** can be attached.
- **Import notes** from a `.txt` or `.docx` file.
- Add a **recording link**.
- Turn on **Share notes with the client** to show them in the portal.

Deleting a meeting sends its notes and files to [Trash](/features/trash).

## Google Calendar sync

Connect Google Calendar from Meetings or your [Profile](/account/profile). Syncing works both ways: Meet links, time changes, and cancellations stay in step. Other Google events appear as **busy** blocks, so you avoid double-booking.
