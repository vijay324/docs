The Leads pipeline is a simple sales board for agencies and freelancers. Every enquiry gets a card, a stage, and a next step.

## Views

Switch between **Board** (drag between stages), **By person**, **Table**, and **Cards**. Flotick remembers your view and page size.

## What a lead holds

Name, company, phone (with country code), email, **stage**, **deal value**, **owner**, **source**, **next follow-up**, star, notes, and any answers from submitted forms.

## Work a lead

- **Log activity**: notes, calls, emails, and meetings.
- **Quick actions**: call, WhatsApp, email, change stage, assign, add a note, schedule a follow-up, or move to Trash.
- **Custom stages** to match how you sell.
- **Reply by email** right from the lead.
- **Mark as lost** to keep your pipeline clean.
- **Convert to client** when you win. You can also create a linked Project in the same step.
- **Start a discovery form, quote, or contract** from the lead. See [Lead forms](/clients/lead-forms) and [Documents](/documents/overview).

## Import and export

Import up to **2,000 leads** from a CSV. Export leads as CSV or JSON.

## Limits and access

| | Free | Starter | Pro | Max |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Open leads | 200 | 3,000 | 25,000 | Unlimited |

Leads from public forms are **always saved**, even over the limit. Members see leads they created or are assigned. Admins and Managers see all.
