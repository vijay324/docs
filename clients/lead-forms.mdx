## Lead capture forms

A lead capture (quote) form turns website visitors into leads automatically. Each submission lands in your [pipeline](/clients/leads).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Leads → Forms and create a form">
    Use the drag-and-drop builder to add fields.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize fields">
    Set labels, placeholders, required fields, dropdown options, and consent text.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Embed it">
    Copy the embed code (script or iframe) and paste it into your website.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Spam protection is built in. The same form can also appear on your [public business page](/grow/business-page).

## Discovery forms

A discovery form is a questionnaire you send to a prospect from their lead. They answer through a private link, with **no account needed**. Answers are saved on the lead. Each link can be submitted **once**.
