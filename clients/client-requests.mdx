A request is anything a client asks of you, or you ask of them: a change, a question, or a missing file. Keeping them together stops things slipping through email.

## Where to find them

Open the client's **Client Portal** tab, or the Project's **Client** tab.

## Status flow

**Open → In review → Planned** or **Closed**.

## Turn a request into work

Choose **Create task** to add a backlog Task from the request. This can be done **once** per request. The client then sees **Added to the plan**, so they know you acted.

<Note>
You need permission to edit the client and to create Tasks.
</Note>

## What the client sees

Clients can raise requests, answer your questions, and upload requested files from the portal. They also see **Seen by the team**. See [Portal requests](/portal/requests).
